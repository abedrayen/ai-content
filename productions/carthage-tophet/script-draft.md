**Format:** 9:16 · target about 55 s · 145–150 words of narration
**Claim IDs** refer to `claims.json`. The wording follows the corrections proposed in `claim-audit.md`.

**Key to narration types:**
- **[FOUND]:** what archaeologists discovered.

## Full narration (for the timed read)
> Two teams studied the same burnt bones from ancient Carthage, and reached conflicting conclusions.
> They come from Carthage's tophet: a sacred precinct where, for some six centuries, people buried urns of cremated infants, and young lambs.
> Stones above many urns record vows to the gods Ba'al Hammon and Tanit.
> Greek writers accused Carthage of sacrificing children. None of them was Carthaginian.
> Patricia Smith's team concluded most of the infants died at one to one and a half months: a pattern they read as sacrifice.
> Jeffrey Schwartz's team concluded many died before or around birth: consistent, they argue, with natural deaths, though occasional sacrifice may have happened.
> The dispute is method: how much fire shrinks bone, and whether it erases a tooth line that forms only after a baby's first week or two.
> One 2024 expert review still called the evidence uncertain. Offered to the gods, or mourned and buried with care? We still don't know.

**About 150 words.** The rendered video runs **56 s**: 59.5 s of scenes (`storyboard-draft.json`) minus 7 crossfades of 0.5 s each. If the timed read is longer than 56 s, cut "for some six centuries" first, then shorten segment 7.

## Wording deliberately avoided
- "Scientists proved…" / "Experts agree…" / "Most experts…" / "Experts are split evenly…"
- "Babies were burned alive…" and any of Diodorus's imagery
- "20,000 children" (c11 is excluded)
- "Moloch"
- Any exact percentage of fetuses or newborns
