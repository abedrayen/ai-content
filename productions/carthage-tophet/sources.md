# Sources: The Tophet of Carthage

Compiled 2026-11-17. Every entry below was located and its bibliographic details checked against the publisher's page or the document itself. **"What was read"** says exactly how much of each source was consulted. Several key papers are paywalled, and for those only the abstract or extract was read. A claim that rests on a source we read only partially is flagged in the evidence register.

Reliability scale: **A** peer-reviewed or primary, read directly · **B** peer-reviewed or scholarly, read only partially · **C** popular, secondary or press, or written by a committed party to the debate · **D** pointer only (not used for claims).

## Discrepancies found between sources

- **Urn counts for the American excavations:** S7 gives both 444 urns and "over 200" in different passages. S20 gives 445. S1 analysed 348, and S2/S4 analysed 334. S9 says these inconsistencies call into question all the quantitative data. **Never narrate a precise urn total.**
- **Urns holding only animals:** S9 reports 25 in Schwartz's data against 59 in Fulton et al. S7 gives 53. **Don't narrate a number.**
- **Date of first use:** Harden's period scheme starts around 800 BC (S7). The Tunisian excavations (S18) report stratigraphy from the 6th century BC in their area. Say "from around the 8th century BC" and attribute it to the excavators.
