A century later, two teams of specialists have studied the same remains — and reached conflicting conclusions. Were the children of Carthage's tophet sacrificed, or mourned and buried with care?

This documentary follows the evidence: the excavations, the ancient Greek accusations, the Punic inscriptions, the skeletal science — and what the evidence can and cannot establish. Interpretations are credited to the scholars who hold them; no side is presented as settled.
